# Code of conduct

Fullstack Forge welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and
viewpoints. Participate with technical rigor and human respect.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Ask precise questions and make room for correction.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorship, and security disclosure boundaries.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate
  disruption.
- Report uncertainty honestly; do not pressure others to hide failures or bypass safety controls.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the security/contact channels on
the GitHub profile. Include only the context needed to investigate. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, pause participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical, with no promise that legal or platform processes can
remain private.

This project-specific policy applies to repository spaces and project-related public interactions.
